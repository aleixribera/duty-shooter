# Refactors

Status: claimed

Spec: `../spec.md`

Split startGame (apps/range/src/main.ts) by flow, move Game's event presentation and HUD-state builders out, one map registry, remove the procedural M4A1 if the Blender one is what plays, dead exports.

## Comments

- One map registry (v2.170.4): `apps/range/src/map/catalogue.ts` holds every map's name, team sizes by mode, whether matches play it, whether its lighting is baked and whether it has a lobby picture. `MapId`, `MAP_IDS`, `TEAM_SIZES`, the bake list in `vite.config.ts`, the lobby pictures in `main.ts` and the map names all derive from it; each builder's `name` reads it. `LOBBY_MAPS` (apps/range) and `BOT_MAPS` (fps-client) were the same three maps under two names and are now `MATCH_MAPS`. The client can't import apps/range, so its copy of the names (`MAP_NAMES`, `lobbyMapName`) is gone and the app injects `mapName` into `Menu`, which passes it to `lobbyTile`. A new map is now its catalogue entry plus its builder; `docs/agents/codebase.md`'s "A map" line says so. `catalogue.test.ts` checks the pictures on disk match the flags and that every match map plays every match mode. Still to do here: split `startGame`, move `Game`'s event presentation and HUD builders out, dead exports, and the procedural M4A1 (held back while ticket 19 changes the M4A1's attachment mounts).
- Dead exports (v2.170.5): ten exports nothing used are gone (`weaponRow`, `sampleVoice`, `TRAP_KINDS`, `BUSES`, `plantCounts`, `MINIMAP_ORIGIN`, `GZ_PIPE_RADIUS`, `GzPiece`, meshkit's `NY` and `vnoise2`), and 105 constants, functions and classes used only in their own file are no longer exported, nor re-exported from the package indexes (`TICK_RATE`, `IW_UNIT`, `ARENA_SCALE`, Charge's and Outposts' tuning numbers among them). Exported types stay: they name parts of signatures a caller may write. Found by a scan of every `export` in `packages/*/src` and `apps/*/src` against every source, test, script and page; the typecheck holds the result.
- Event presentation's words (v2.170.6): what each event tells the player (objective feed lines, kill feed rows, the eliminated callout, toasts) is now `eventWords(event, context)` in `packages/fps-client/src/hud/event-words.ts`, a pure function with its own test of who is told what (a defender isn't told who carries the charge, only your side hears a revive, a dropped Reboot Card is told only while you live). `Game.#present` keeps the sounds, effects and match state, and puts the words on the HUD after its switch; it builds the context only for events that can say anything (`WORDY`), not for every shot. Moving the rest of `#present` and the HUD-state builders out wholesale would hand another class some thirty of `Game`'s fields; the next seams worth cutting are the HUD-state builders (`#royaleHud`, `#chargeHud`, `#spectatorPanel`, `#matchCard`) the same way, pure functions over a small context.
- HUD-state builders (v2.170.7): what the Battle Royale HUD, the Charge bar and the match card show is now `royaleHudState`, `chargeHudState` and `matchCardState` in `packages/fps-client/src/hud/hud-state.ts`, pure functions over a small context (the match, your soldier, who you watch, the keys to name, and a `project` callback for the Charge markers' screen positions). `hud-state.test.ts` holds who sees what: a defender gets no marker for a dropped charge, the plant replaces the site markers, out of the fight the map stands where the one you watch stands and leaves them off your mates, a late arrival has no seat and no match card, the card waits for the victory beat and counts down through the scoreboard. `Game` keeps thin wrappers that gather the context. `#spectatorPanel` stays in `Game`: it holds the last panel across frames for a player not sent yet, and its notice and recap timers, so cutting it would mean passing that state in and out for little gain. Checked in a Charge bot match: site markers with distances, the bar, and "You carry the charge". Still to do here: split `startGame`, and the procedural M4A1 (held for ticket 19).
- `startGame`, first cut (v2.170.8): the pieces of `apps/range/src/main.ts` that stand on their own are modules now. `map-store.ts` (`MapStore`: each map built once with its lighting downloading, and its bot routes from a worker), `bot-setup.ts` (`BotSetup`: Bot Match's picks, with `bot-setup.test.ts` holding that a size of your own lasts until the map or mode changes and that a side keeps 1 to `MAX_BOTS_PER_TEAM` bots), `local-bots.ts` (Local bots' worker), `lobby-status.ts` (the status and `/healthz` calls, the seat key) and `benchmark-run.ts` (`startBenchmarkOn` and the benchmark page's run over every map). `main.ts` is 1,076 lines, down from 1,318. What stays in `startGame` is the flows that share the page's state (the game on show, the menu, the lobby and Battle Royale pages, the join attempt): Play Online, the Battle Royale screen, the Preparation and the per-frame lobby. The next cut there is the Preparation (`updatePreparation` and its four `prep*` variables) as a class over the game and its page. Checked in the browser: a Bot Match (setup, Free-for-all, Start, a match on Survival Town with six players), the benchmark page (five maps, 205 s), and a Local bots Battle Royale up to its lobby with the worker's stats. Still to do here: the rest of `startGame`, and the procedural M4A1 (held for ticket 19).
