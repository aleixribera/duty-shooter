## Comments

**Pass 1 (loop pass 8, 2026-11-13): spawns look into the map, not into a wall.** Review `../reviews/2026-11-13a.md` (its first reading) fails objective 3. Its gaps by map: Survival Town's last minute barely reaches the sky (the reviewer's "fix first"); Switchyard's spawns face a brick wall point-blank (20-a, 23-a); Station Nine gives a kill 2 s in; the range stalls 4.8 s. I fixed the Switchyard spawns first, not the sky. Aleix played the last minute and said it lands ("Hell yeah it slaps", spec.md), while a wall in the face greets every life on every map, and the review counts it among its five most visible defects. The sky is next (pass 2 of this item).

Measured first: at eye height, 27 spawns on the three match maps looked into a wall within 8 m along the crosshair (sets counted apart). Switchyard had 19, among them 12 of its 18 team end spawns (4.2 to 7.0 m: the screens that keep the two ends from seeing each other stand 5 m in front of them). Station Nine had one (the spawn between the bunker and the road, 4.5 m) and Survival Town seven (5.0 to 6.7 m).

Fix: `apps/range/src/map/spawn-facing.ts`. At map build, a spawn whose crosshair line meets a wall within 8 m, or the play area's edge (Switchyard's boundary is low enough to see the desert over), turns by the smallest 5° step, up to 60°, whose ±4° of view sees past 8 m. A spawn that already sees past 8 m keeps the facing its map gave it, so Survival Town's back yards still face their houses and Station Nine's end spawns still aim through their gaps. Windows don't count as walls (as `sightBlocked`). The server builds maps through the same builders, so it spawns players with the same yaws. It adds 1-2 ms to a map build.

Checked: `apps/range/test/spawn-facing.test.ts` (the least turn, glass, the play area's edge, and every spawn on the three match maps). Its map test fails on HEAD's maps with seven Survival Town spawns at 5.0 to 6.7 m, Switchyard's and Station Nine's likewise. The maps' own spawn tests pass, as do `bot-match.test.ts` and the typecheck. Frames before and after: `../spawn-view/sheet.jpg` (`node .scratch/next-level/spawn-view/look.mjs <out> <map> name=x,z,oldYaw ...`). Switchyard's north-west spawn now looks down the yard instead of at a brick hut; the next two look through the gap between hut and container; Station Nine's road spawn looks down the road instead of at the bunker's wall. One spawn stays weak: Switchyard (-49, 12), and its mirror, have no open view east within 60°, so they turn to a building's wall about 9 m off, with its door. Moving them is a layout change, left for the next review's verdict.

**Pass 2 (loop pass 9, 2026-11-13): the last minute reaches the world.** The review's "fix first": from T-1:00 to T-0:03 the sky, light and buildings didn't change; only the clock turned red. The amber grade (ticket 24) is a grade that keeps the brightest parts' own colour, so the sky, the brightest thing on screen, stayed pale. Aleix likes the last minute, so this adds to it and changes none of it: the grade, the beat, the calls and the siren are as they were.

What changed: `lastMinuteLighting(base, sky)` in `last-minute.ts` turns a map's own light toward the minute's end (sky zenith to rust, horizon haze to burnt orange, sky 25 % dimmer, sun to red-orange and 30 % weaker, cloud cover up 0.3), and `lastMinuteSky` makes that turn run evenly with the clock (the grade comes fastest at the start; the sky keeps darkening into the last ten seconds). `WorldView.setLastMinute` puts it into the shared light uniforms (`showLighting`, which leaves `currentLighting()` as the map's own) and the sun light for soldiers and props, and the gun's own lights follow (`Viewmodel.takeLight`). Every world material, the haze and the drawn sky read those uniforms, so it costs no shader and no frame time. The sky holds through the ending and the scoreboard (the mushroom cloud stands under a rust sky), and clears over two seconds after them or at once on leaving. Charge and Battle Royale have no such clock and get none of it. The siren was already there (`countdownSirenRise` in the review's sound log), so nothing was added to the sound.

Checked: `last-minute.test.ts` (the even turn, darker and redder each second down, the map's own object at nothing, a dusk map turned the same way), the tests related to the changed files (739 pass), typecheck. The review's check, measured on frames from one view (`../last-sky/look.mjs`, a 70 s Survival Town Bot Match): the sky sample at x 1200-1500, y 150-250 went from luminance 172.9 at T-0:55 to 145.0 at 30 s, 114.7 at 10 s and 97.6 at 3 s (a fall of 75, the check asks 25), with RGB 134, 91, 58 at 3 s (red over green and blue). Before (review 2026-11-13a): 189 at 10 s and at 3 s, the same. Frames: `../last-sky/sheet.jpg` (before against after), `../last-sky/after-hold/end-9-1920.jpg` (the scoreboard under the held sky).

Second opinion (one review subagent on the diff): no leaks into the bake, the reflections, the menu backdrop or a new map. Fixed: a light another view might put up during the held ending would have stuck (the early return now also checks the uniforms still hold this view's light); the gun stayed in daylight (now it takes the turned light); the cloud clamp could have thinned a cloudier map's sky. It also reported an older bug outside this diff: the spare Practice Range game's view, built while the menu shows, puts up the range's light, and when it later takes over after another map was built, its own light may not be put back. Not verified; noted under Broken in STATE.

**Pass 3 (loop pass 17, 2026-11-13): a free-for-all doesn't open on a fight.** Review 2026-11-13b: First Blood at T-9:59 on Station Nine in both Free-for-all and Gun Game ("spawn fights still start in the first second"). Two causes, measured with `../first-blood/probe.test.ts` (bots only, 48 seeded matches per row): (1) at the start every player was placed one at a time, each blind to those placed after it, so pairs of enemies stood in each other's sight at T0 (Survival Town 6 players: 82 pairs over 48 matches; Station Nine 68); (2) the 1.5 s of spawn protection started at the spawn, inside the 5 s held countdown, so it had always run out before play began.

Fix: `openingSpawns` (`fps-core/src/modes/spawns.ts`) deals a free-for-all's (and gun game's) opening spawns together: as few pairs in sight (or within 8 m) as the map allows, then as far apart as can be, drawn among near-equal deals so matches vary. Later spawns follow the old rules; no deal when players outnumber spawns. The server's spawn protection now counts from when play begins for anyone placed in the countdown (`#spawnProtected`), so a player who doesn't fire can't be shot dead in the first 1.5 s.

Numbers (`../first-blood/numbers.txt`, `after-final.txt`): pairs in sight at T0 for 6 players fall to each map's least (Station Nine 68 to 48, one a match, the fewest its 14 spawns allow; Survival Town 82 to 15; Switchyard 0). First kill, median: Switchyard 3.4 s to 4.4 s, Station Nine 2.3 s to 2.7 s (under 2 s: 12 to 8 of 48), Survival Town 1.7 s to 1.9 s. At 12 players (Bot Match's full lobby) pairs in sight fall by a fifth to a half, but bots open fire at once, which ends their own protection, and the first kill still lands at 1.3-1.7 s: 12 players on 14-18 spawns is crowded. More FFA spawns per map is a layout change, in BACKLOG. Seen in the game: three Station Nine Free-for-all Bot Matches (`../first-blood/after/`, `look.mjs`), no enemy in view at T0, first kill in the feed at 1.6-1.9 s, no page errors.

Second opinion (one review subagent): pinning protection to `#matchStart` at spawn time broke in Battle Royale, whose start moves when everyone readies early (up to 15 s of protection after landing) or waits on a loading page (protection expiring in the countdown again); now the stored time stays the spawn's and `#spawnProtected` measures from the later of it and the current start. It also found the deal could seat two players 3 m apart across a wall; pairs within 8 m now count as seen. Both have tests that fail without them.

Pass 19 (2026-11-13), Zero Island's boxy cliffs, pass 1: cut, nothing shipped. The draft generator and fit setting are in `../strata/sandstone.patch` (applies to `tools/map/vegetation.py` and `tools/map/fit-solids.mjs` at v2.194.1). Options: shallow notches with the relief kept to the beds' tops and corners; one box per bed (about seven) fitted to each bed's narrowest point, with the lip ledges kept under 0.25 m; or a ledge model and a pillar model so neither is stretched 13 m.
