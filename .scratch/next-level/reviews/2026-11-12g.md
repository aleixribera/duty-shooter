## The 5 most visible defects

1. **01, right two-thirds (x 776-1920, y 0-1080):** the desktop main menu has no backdrop. Where review f's 01 showed the aerial of Survival Town, this frame is a flat near-black navy field with faint vertical banding (mean brightness about 18 of 255, against 139 in review f's frame); the destination list on the left is answered by nothing on the right. The phone frame taken 0.8 s later (02) has the aerial, so the menu reveals before its world has drawn.
2. **05, centre and upper left:** two particles cross the view as giant flat cards: a grey lopsided diamond about 65 x 420 px standing straight over the crosshair (x 905-970, y 258-680), and a grey blade slanting through the stencil "0" (x 655-775, y 150-325). They are untextured, unlit mid-grey, larger than the gun, and read as cardboard rather than wood chips. They are the shard particles (`effects.ts`, kind `chip`) thrown off the wall, with no limit on how near the camera they may pass.
3. **07, just right of and under the crosshair (x 972-1036, y 470-552):** two ENEMY markers stack on each other: the second's red triangle sits on the first label's "M", the second label covers the first's distance, and only one "20 m" shows. At the centre of the screen a player can't tell how many enemies there are or which is nearer. Beside them two ally chevrons (x 843 and 880, y 497) carry no name.
4. **03, bottom right (x 1318-1838, y 818-842):** the new Bot skill row is cut by the footer: only the top half of EASY, NORMAL and HARD shows, with NORMAL's lit frame sliced at its middle, and a scrollbar at x 1897 is the only sign there is more. At 1904x929 the setup's last pick needs a scroll and reads as a layout fault.
5. **08, right of centre (x 1172-1448, y 538-608):** the desert bunker is an untextured smooth beige box with a flat grey door (x 1203-1226) and no frame; its four window slits (x 1302-1410, y 559-567) are ragged black blots with stair-stepped edges, not openings. It is the only building in a wide, otherwise good frame (cracked desert floor, haze on the range) and the eye goes to it.
