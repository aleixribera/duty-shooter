Weakest point: picture at arm's length. Three of the five play frames (05, 07, 08) put a surface within two metres of the eye, and each of those surfaces is a flat fill: the tangerine tread undersides in 05, the plain ochre wall in 07 (and 06), the blurred bus lettering in 08. A player spends much of a match against cover, so these are what the game looks like most of the time. Fix in under an hour: take the three spots from these frames (the north house's stair foot, the room in 06/07, the bus side) and fix the worst one first, the stair underside: the downward faces of the treads and stringer under the deck should come out at the shade level of the deck's own underside (05, top left, the dark brown boards), not at the level of a sunlit face. Check with a still from the 05 spot against 04's view of the same stairs.

## The 5 most visible defects

1. **05, top right quadrant:** the undersides of the stair treads and the long stringer crossing the frame are a flat, saturated tangerine, as bright as a face in full sun, though they face the ground; with only thin ruled seams on them they read as orange plastic boards. The same stairs seen from above in 04 are dark stained wood, and the deck's underside in 05 (top left) is a dark brown.
2. **05, right half:** two ejected casings (about x 1590-1630, y 575-640 and x 1785-1870, y 630-660) are brown rods with a hot white-yellow outline along their edges, like glowsticks, the brightest things in the frame after the muzzle flash; a third (x 1255-1320, y 648) is a dull brown stick. None of them reads as brass.
3. **08, bottom third:** the "SCHOOL BUS" letters along the bus side are blurred black blocks with stair-stepped notches in their edges, and the bullet holes in the black letters (x 260 y 780, x 555 y 690, x 700 y 810) show as small pale tan four-pointed stars, not holes.
4. **07, whole frame (and 06 behind the board):** the tan wall the player faces is one flat ochre fill with no grain, texture or variation edge to edge; the only pattern on it is in the sunlit wedge at the top left (x 240-440, y 0-300), a fine diagonal dot grid like a screen door.
5. **04, left third:** the ground-floor window (x 190-345, y 420-500) is two flat mid-grey panes with no sky, reflection or room behind them, while the open doorway beside it (x 620-715) shows a lit room with a sofa.
