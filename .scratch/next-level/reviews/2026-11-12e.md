1. **08, upper right (and 07, centre):** the bullet holes in the mint siding each sit in a pale yellow-green fuzzy disc two to three times the hole's width; a cluster of six reads as moss or lily-pad stickers on the wall, not as chipped paint around a hole.
2. **06, behind the scoreboard:** the school bus's windows and the red pickup's windscreen are flat black slabs with no glass, reflection or interior, and a round white smudge floats on the pickup's windscreen (centre-right).
3. **05, top right:** the white handrail along the deck stairs blooms into a glowing beam from the top centre to the right edge, with a hot spot where it meets the stringer (centre-right, by Rook's tag); it reads as light leaking through a seam.
4. **05, centre-right:** the red ENEMY 53 m marker and its triangle sit directly on Rook's ally chevron, so the two markers merge and the triangle hides the chevron.
5. **07, bottom centre:** the hint "Q Swap to your other weapon: faster than a reload" shows while the magazine is full (30, bottom right), the one moment it can't help.
