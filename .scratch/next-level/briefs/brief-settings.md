## Ticket 22: settings and quality tiers

No graphics control exists: everybody gets the same picture and a weak laptop gets a slideshow. Build:

- **Quality tiers** (Low, Medium, High, Ultra, and Auto as the default): each tier a table, in one place, of what it turns on and at what cost: render scale, shadow map size and distance, GTAO, TAA or its cheaper alternative, light shafts, bloom, particles' count, grass/scatter density and draw distance, texture anisotropy, the sky field, anything else in `renderer.ts` / `pipeline.ts` / the views that costs frame time. Find what actually costs by measuring (`npm run bench` and the scripts in `apps/range/scripts/`; `docs/agents/codebase.md` says how), not by guessing; write the table of frame time per tier on each map into the ticket.
- **Dynamic resolution**: a controller that holds a target frame time (the display's refresh, or a chosen cap) by moving the render scale between a floor and 100 %, with hysteresis so it does not pump; the HUD stays at native resolution; upscaled output is sharpened so it does not go soft. Auto picks a tier from a short measurement on the first run and from the adapter's hints, and remembers it.
- A frame-rate cap setting, a V-sync note where the browser decides, and the FPS counter showing the render scale when dynamic resolution is active.
- **Other settings the ticket names**: crosshair options (shape, size, gap, thickness, colour from a small fixed set, dot, and whether it spreads with movement), mouse invert Y, a camera-motion amount (0 to 100 %: bob, roll, landing dip, weapon sway; 0 must be safe for motion-sick players) and an FOV-effects amount (sprint FOV kick, damage and explosion shake), colour-blind team colours (the two team colours and the enemy marker colour swapped for sets that survive deuteranopia, protanopia and tritanopia: everywhere a team colour is drawn, in the world and in the HUD, from one source), HUD scale (80 to 130 %), and a subtitle/caption option for the announcer's lines. The five audio sliders already exist (master, music, effects, voice, UI): keep them and lay them out with the rest.
- **The Settings screen itself**: with this many settings it needs sections (Controls, Mouse and pad, Video, Audio, HUD and crosshair, Accessibility), a live preview where it helps (the crosshair; the team colours), "Restore defaults" per section, keyboard, pad and touch navigation, and it must work at 390x844 as well as 1920x1080. Follow `DESIGN.md` (instrument-panel look; no centred hero, icon cards, gradients or pill badges) and use its tokens. Old saved settings must keep working (new fields get defaults when missing).
