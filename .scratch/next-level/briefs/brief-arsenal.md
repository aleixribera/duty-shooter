## The job: four new weapons, finished

The game has five weapons (M4A1 rifle, Wasp 9 SMG, a shotgun, a sniper, a pistol). Add four, each with its own role in a fight, so nine guns feel like nine choices:

1. **A light machine gun**: big belt or box, slow to raise and reload, heavy, holds an angle; recoil that settles as the burst goes on rather than growing.
2. **A marksman rifle**: semi-automatic, two shots to kill at range with one to the head, between the rifle and the sniper; a low-power optic or good irons.
3. **A burst rifle**: three-round burst, one burst kills when all three land, a delay between bursts that punishes a miss.
4. **A revolver**: a secondary; six rounds, slow, hits like a rifle up close; a reload that is its own (cylinder out, rounds in).

Names are this game's own, in the style of the ones there (look at how the existing weapons are named in `packages/fps-core/src/stats/weapons.ts` and the glossary; no real-world trademarks beyond what the game already uses). ## Protocol and codec

`packages/fps-core/src/net/codec.ts` has a one-byte word dictionary `WORDS` that is FULL (224 of 224). Never add to it. New words (weapon ids, a fire mode name) go at the END of `MORE_WORDS` in the same file (two bytes each); the order of both lists is part of the protocol. If you add words or change what is sent, raise `PROTOCOL_VERSION` in `net/protocol.ts` by one and say so in your report.
