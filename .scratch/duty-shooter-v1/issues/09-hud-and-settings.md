# 09 HUD, range overlay and settings menu

Status: resolved
Milestone: 3 (audio, effects, HUD, graphics presets)
Blocked by: 06

## What to build

HUD (51-57): hipfire crosshair hidden on ADS, hit markers with a red kill marker and a headshot variant, directional damage indicator, ammo counter, kill feed component, toggleable ping and fps counter, and the range overlay with time-to-kill, distance to target and recoil trace. A full settings menu: rebinding, hold/toggle ADS and crouch, sensitivity, ADS multiplier, ADS FOV affected/independent, FOV, graphics preset, overlay toggles.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Playtester agent: every HUD element appears in the right situation; rebinding a key works and persists across a reload.
