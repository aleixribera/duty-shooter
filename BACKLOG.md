On the laptop's Intel UHD at 1080p the benchmark runs 23 to 29 fps (GPU 14 to 22 ms in the passes, frame 35 to 44 ms in Firefox). The previous-depth copy each frame is the same kind of hidden cost: the world pass drawing into two depth textures in turn would drop it. Each measured with `toaster/laptop-toggle.mjs` in one page on the laptop (prefer `flag:` switches, ticket 99). The CPU side (7 to 11 ms of script a frame, and Firefox's own presenting) wants Chrome on the laptop to measure.
