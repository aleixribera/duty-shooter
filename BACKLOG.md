- **The viewmodel's composite treats its MSAA edges as straight alpha** (pass 45's second opinion). `pipeline.ts` lays the viewmodel over the world as `mix(world, vm.rgb, vm.a)`, but a resolved 4x edge against a clear pixel is premultiplied, so edges come out dark (k^2 c instead of k c); a sight's reticle meets it now that its window is clear. `world*(1 - vm.a) + vm.rgb` is right for opaque edges; check additive effects (the muzzle flash, lens glass) before changing it.
