# duty-shooter design audit, 2026-10-28

Captured at 112d9f3 (with uncommitted changes): 14 shots (desktop, phone). Contact sheet: [2026-10-28/sheet.html](2026-10-28/sheet.html) · before/after: [2026-10-28/compare.html](2026-10-28/compare.html) · contrast table: [2026-10-28/captures/contrast.md](2026-10-28/captures/contrast.md).

<!-- summary:start -->
**Summary.** The menus share one solid design system (one font, a mint accent, team colours with marks, key hints on every screen), and most menu text passes contrast. The weak spots are the in-play HUD and the first minutes:

1. **The HUD corners have no backing (F01).** HEALTH, the weapon name and ammo are pale text straight on the 3D scene, so over sunlit grass or sky the labels fade out and the big numbers lose punch. A soft dark gradient or plate behind each corner fixes the HUD contrast findings at once (F01; F11's see-through score plate is the same problem at the top).
2. **A first-time player gets no help (F02, F13, F03).** The range spawns you with no controls hint and no task, next to an empty stats panel. The match goal is the code "TDM · 75". The main menu's most prominent option, Play Online, is unavailable offline. A one-time controls card, a plain-language goal line ("First team to 75 kills wins"), and making Bot Match the primary option when offline would cover most first-time findings.
3. **Keyboard/gamepad focus (F05).** Settings and the lobby open with nothing focused, though the footer advertises arrow keys, and the main menu's focus marker is a thin teal edge. Focusing the first item on open, with a stronger marker, makes the advertised controls true.

The phone viewport is a stress test (the game is desktop-first). It shows overflow on the settings tabs, the scoreboard and the pause overlay (F07, F09, F16, F17), worth fixing only if phone or narrow windows matter. Not covered by these captures: damage, low ammo, death, round end and objective modes (Outposts, Charge). Add those states to `design-audit.json` once there are debug hooks to force them.

## Details

### F01 · Health and ammo readouts wash out over bright grass and sky

- **Where:** match-hud @ desktop, box [28, 972, 84, 72] in [match-hud@desktop.png](2026-10-28/captures/match-hud%40desktop.png)
- **Rule:** COL-01, Text contrast over the real background
- **Severity:** high · **Lens:** accessibility, rules, hierarchy, hud
- **What:** The bottom-corner HUD has only a faint text shadow and no plate, so over the light-green lawn of Survival Town the HEALTH and AUTO labels effectively disappear and the big 100 / 30 numbers lose their edge. A player checking ammo or health mid-fight has to look twice. The same thing happens on the scoreboard shot (identical numbers) and in the practice range over the sand floor (Health 2.27:1, 100 at p10 2.82:1).
- **Evidence:** contrast.json match-hud@desktop, real pixels: 'Health' 12px p50 1.09 / p10 1.03 (needs 4.5); 'auto' 10px bold p50 1.06; 'M4A1' 15px bold p50 1.64; '/ ∞' 20px p50 1.78; '100' 48px bold p50 2.52 / p10 1.81 and '30' 54px bold p50 2.66 / p10 1.76 (need 3:1). Zoomed crop confirms grey #B4BAC5 'HEALTH' over pale green is barely visible. Ammo cluster box [1746,969,142,72]. · contrast.md match-hud@desktop p10: 'Health' 1.03 (needs 4.5), '100' 1.81 and '30' 1.76 (need 3 at 48/54 px bold), 'M4A1' 1.60, '/ ∞' 1.47, 'auto' 1.03. Only a text shadow, no backing plate. The ammo cluster sits at [1746,969,142,72].
- **Fix:** Put a soft dark gradient scrim behind both bottom corners (e.g. a linear-gradient from rgba(8,10,14,0.55) at the bottom edge to transparent, ~140 px tall), add a 1-2 px dark outline or a tighter, stronger text-shadow (0 0 3px rgba(0,0,0,0.8)) to all HUD text, and raise the secondary labels from #B4BAC5 to near-white. Re-measure until labels reach 4.5:1 and the numbers 3:1 at p10 on this shot.

### F02 · First spawn in the range shows no controls, no task, and an empty stats panel as the biggest HUD block

- **Where:** range-hud @ desktop, box [37, 808, 181, 149] in [range-hud@desktop.png](2026-10-28/captures/range-hud%40desktop.png)
- **Rule:** ONB-01, Controls are discoverable in context
- **Severity:** medium · **Lens:** first-time, hud, rules, hierarchy
- **What:** A newcomer drops into the Practice Range with a rifle and nothing telling them how to move, aim down sights, reload or what to try first. The full key list exists, but only on the pause screen, and nothing on the HUD says Esc opens it. The match HUD's first spawn has the same gap. The stats panel's lower half is an empty dark box, so it reads as broken or not loaded rather than as a place for guidance.
- **Evidence:** HUD text is only "TTK", "DISTANCE 25.0 m", "SHOTS / HITS", "LAST HIT", "HEALTH 100", "M4A1 AUTO 30 / ∞"; empty panel below; controls only appear under "CONTROLS" in the pause menu
- **Fix:** On first spawn, show a dismissible hint card (in the empty panel area or bottom centre): "WASD move · Mouse aim · LMB fire · RMB aim · R reload · Esc controls & menu", plus a one-line task such as "Shoot the 25 m dummy, then try the SPRINT/WALK course". Switch the glyphs when a gamepad is active. Drop the empty box or fill it with that hint.

### F03 · The top menu option is unavailable but looks as clickable as the rest

- **Where:** main-menu @ desktop, box [56, 644, 480, 100] in [main-menu@desktop.png](2026-10-28/captures/main-menu%40desktop.png)
- **Rule:** MENU-01, One obvious next step
- **Severity:** medium · **Lens:** first-time, hierarchy, rules
- **What:** The first and most prominent option, PLAY ONLINE, is the one a newcomer reaches for. It is styled like the working options, and the only warning is a small red dot and a technical sentence. A player is likely to click it, hit a dead end, and only then try Bot Match, which already has focus but doesn't otherwise stand out.
- **Evidence:** "PLAY ONLINE / No game server answers this page right now. / ● OFFLINE" in the same card style as "BOT MATCH", "PRACTICE RANGE", "SETTINGS" · Four tiles 480 px wide with identical 28 px/700 off-white titles (243,241,234) and the same dark fill. Play Online's only offline cue is a 12 px 'OFFLINE' label with a red dot at [144,718,60,12] under the description 'No game server answers this page right now.' The Bot Match tile [56,751,480,95] differs only by its left accent bar.
- **Fix:** Dim the Play Online card as disabled (lower opacity, no hover) with plain wording such as "Online play is unavailable right now", or move it below Bot Match while offline. Give the recommended option (Bot Match, or Practice Range for a first launch) a filled primary style.
- **After:** FAIL: Not addressed by this trial fix: Play Online still looks as available as Bot Match.

### F04 · The section tabs are parked far from the settings they switch, so KEYS, VIDEO and INTERFACE are easy to miss

### F05 · Menus open with nothing focused although the footer offers arrow-key navigation, and the focus marker is a thin edge

### F06 · Slider hit areas are 20 px tall and tabs sit 2 px apart

- **Where:** settings @ desktop, box [620, 182, 270, 529] in [settings@desktop.png](2026-10-28/captures/settings%40desktop.png)
- **Rule:** MENU-03, Controls are big enough to hit
- **Severity:** medium · **Lens:** rules
- **What:** All six sensitivity and deadzone sliders have a 20 px tall hit area, below the ~32 px mouse minimum, so a slightly-off click misses or grabs the wrong row. The tabs are only 2 px apart. In the lobby, the Mode and Sides segmented buttons are 1 px apart. On the phone the same screen has 11 targets under 44 px (tabs 40 px, selects 38 px, sliders 20 px).
- **Evidence:** Controls 'Mouse sensitivity', 'ADS sensitivity multiplier', 'Horizontal/Vertical stick sensitivity', 'Controller ADS multiplier', 'Stick deadzone': input 270x20. - **Fix:** Make each range input at least 32 px tall (keep the thin track visual and enlarge the hit area with padding or height), let the whole row forward clicks to the slider, and put 8 px gaps between tabs and segmented options (or merge each segmented group into one control with arrow-key switching).
