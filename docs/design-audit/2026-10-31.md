No finding comes from the performance work. The screens that lost their fades read the same, and the menu's first paint on its plain dark background (taken by hand, not in these captures) is complete at desktop and phone width, with nothing moving when the world appears behind it.

Fix first:

1. **Phone is not playable (F03, F07).** There are no touch controls, so a phone player reaches a match and can't move, aim or fire. Everything else on phone (F01, F08, F09, F10, F36, F37) matters only once this exists. Either add a touch layer or say on the menu that the game needs a keyboard and mouse.
2. **The HUD washes out over bright ground (F04, F05, F06).** Health, weapon and the score strip's labels measure 1.06 to 3.44:1 on Survival Town's grass. A dark plate or stronger corner scrim fixes all three; the kill target is the worst of them.
3. **First spawn explains nothing (F02, F29, F30).** No controls and no task are shown, and "change your loadout now" gives an order with no way to carry it out.

Not covered: Play Online's lobby, the death and round-end states, Crash and Switchyard HUDs, and the desktop app window.

### F02 · First spawn shows no controls and no task

- **Where:** range-hud @ desktop, box [840, 530, 240, 70] in [range-hud@desktop.png](2026-10-31/captures/range-hud%40desktop.png)
- **Rule:** ONB-01, Controls are discoverable in context
- **Severity:** high · **Lens:** first-time
- **What:** A new player lands facing a dummy with a gun in hand and nothing telling them which keys do what, what to do here, or how to leave. Move, aim and fire are guessable for FPS players; aim down sights, grenade, prone, mantle and the Esc menu are not. The controls list exists but only appears if they think to press Esc, and nothing on screen mentions Esc. match-hud has the same gap.
- **Evidence:** The range-hud@desktop DOM has 15 text runs and every one is a stat or ammo value: none is a key name, a verb or an exit hint. The only instruction in the scene is world signs reading "25 m", "50 m", "SPRINT", "WALK". The client has no first-run prompt text: no "press" or "click to" string in hud/ or the in-play code.
- **Fix:** On first spawn, show a dismissable strip along the bottom edge: "WASD move · Mouse aim · Left click fire · Right click aim · R reload · Esc menu", plus a one-line task such as "Shoot the dummies, then walk the lanes". Fade it after the player has moved and fired.

### F03 · A phone player gets into the game and has no way to move, aim or fire

- **Where:** range-hud @ phone, box [0, 500, 390, 344] in [range-hud@phone.png](2026-10-31/captures/range-hud%40phone.png)
- **Rule:** ONB-01, Controls are discoverable in context
- **Severity:** high · **Lens:** first-time
- **What:** Past the menus, a touch player sees the HUD and nothing to press: no stick, no fire button, no reload, nothing to open the pause menu or scoreboard. The same applies on match-hud@phone. If they do reach the pause screen, its Controls panel teaches WASD, Left mouse and Shift Left, and Settings opens on Mouse, so the only help describes hardware they don't have.
- **Evidence:** range-hud@phone and match-hud@phone: controls [] in the .dom.json, though the capture ran with hasTouch and isMobile. packages/fps-client/src/input.ts listens only for keydown, mousedown, mousemove, wheel and pointer lock (plus gamepad); there are no touch handlers anywhere in the client. pause@phone quotes "WASD", "Left mouse", "Right mouse", "Control Left", "Rebind in Settings", and the list runs off the bottom at Tab (y 834).
- **Fix:** Add touch controls: a left move stick, right-side drag to look, and fire, aim, reload, jump, pause and scoreboard buttons, with the pause Controls panel showing those on touch devices. Until they exist, tell phone players on the main menu that the game needs a keyboard and mouse or a controller, before they enter a match.

### F07 · A phone player gets no touch controls, so the match cannot be played

- **Where:** match-hud @ phone, box [0, 420, 390, 424] in [match-hud@phone.png](2026-10-31/captures/match-hud%40phone.png)
- **Rule:** NEW
- **Severity:** high · **Lens:** accessibility, hud, rules
- **What:** New idea: every input a mode needs must exist on every viewport the game offers. On a phone the HUD has nothing to move, aim, fire, reload or pause with, so a match starts and the player can only watch.
