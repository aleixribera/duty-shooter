### F01 · A phone player is never told the game can't be played by touch

- **Where:** main-menu @ phone, box [16, 418, 358, 370] in [main-menu@phone.png](2026-11-05/captures/main-menu%40phone.png)
- **Rule:** NEW
- **Severity:** high · **Lens:** first-time
- **What:** On a phone the whole menu works: they can type a name, open Bot Match and press START. But the game has no touch controls (no joystick or touch handlers in fps-client), so they reach a match they can't move or shoot in. Nothing on the menu, in the lobby or in Settings says a keyboard and mouse or a controller is needed. Settings even opens on MOUSE and CONTROLLER sections, which suggests a phone isn't expected but never says so.
- **Evidence:** main-menu@phone.png: all five entries are enabled with no device note; settings@phone.png opens on 'Mouse sensitivity'. Grep for touchstart/joystick in packages/fps-client/src finds nothing.
- **Fix:** On touch-only devices, show a one-line notice under the title ('Needs a keyboard and mouse or a controller') in place of the footer hint. Keep the menus browsable but repeat the notice on START, rather than dropping the player into a match they can't control.

### F02 · On phone the mode, map and sides choices are hidden below a 12-row player list and the sticky footer

- **Where:** bot-lobby @ phone, box [16, 140, 358, 578] in [bot-lobby@phone.png](2026-11-05/captures/bot-lobby%40phone.png)
- **Rule:** NEW
- **Severity:** high · **Lens:** accessibility, first-time, hierarchy
- **What:** The phone lobby's first screen shows only the header, the seat rows and a sticky footer with START. The whole MATCH section (mode, map cards, Random, sides) is below the fold, and nothing hints that it's there. A phone player can start a match without seeing which map or mode they picked. The last seat rows also scroll under the footer: the measured contrast of 'Open seat 12' (1.3:1) comes from it being hidden behind the footer, not from its colour.
- **Evidence:** bot-lobby@phone.dom.json lists no Match/Mode/Map texts or controls in the viewport; its only controls are BACK/LOADOUT/SETTINGS at y=718 (44 tall) and START [16,774,358,58]. contrast.md bot-lobby@phone: '12' [31,775,11,13] p50 1.3 FAIL, 'Open seat' [67,773,71,16] p50 1.3 FAIL, 'Open seat' [67,721,71,16] p10 2.12 warn, all under the footer. · bot-lobby@phone.png: the visible area is the player list plus BACK/LOADOUT/SETTINGS/START; the DOM texts stop at 'Open seat' 12 and the hint at [16,822]. · DOM sample: the last visible content is 'Bots fill the match to 4v4...' at y=822; no Mode/Map/Seats text is in the viewport. Rows 01-10 occupy y=187-700, footer with Start at y=774.
- **Fix:** On narrow screens, put the Match summary card (Mode / Map / Seats) above the player list, or collapse the seat list to a 'You + 7 bots, 4 open' summary row that expands. Show the chosen map and mode on the START button or right above it.
- **After:** PASS: Match block (readouts, maps, mode, sides) now comes before the seat list on phone

### F03 · On a phone the match setup is off-screen and the footer covers the last seats

- **Where:** bot-lobby @ phone, box [16, 712, 358, 132] in [bot-lobby@phone.png](2026-11-05/captures/bot-lobby%40phone.png)

### F04 · Nothing has focus when a screen opens, so keyboard and gamepad players can't tell what Enter will do

- **Where:** error @ desktop, box [80, 964, 370, 44] in [error@desktop.png](2026-11-05/captures/error%40desktop.png)
- **Rule:** MENU-04, Focus is always visible
- **Severity:** high · **Lens:** accessibility
- **What:** The `focused` field is null in all 10 DOM samples. On the Connection-failed dialog, TRY AGAIN and MAIN MENU look like a filled button and an outlined one, with no focus mark on either, so a pad player can't tell which one A or Enter will trigger. Settings and the Bot Match lobby are the same: their footers promise '↑↓ MOVE / ENTER SELECT', but no control is marked. The only highlight in the lobby is the teal-bordered '01 You' row, which is a player marker and can't be clicked, so it looks like focus when it isn't.
- **Evidence:** error@desktop.dom.json: focused=null; controls TRY AGAIN [80,964,180,44] and MAIN MENU [270,964,180,44] are drawn with no focus ring. bot-lobby@desktop.dom.json: focused=null, while the only outlined element is the '01 You' row at about [56,192,608,52]. settings@desktop.dom.json: focused=null, and no row has a focus treatment (only the active-tab underline).
- **Fix:** When each screen opens, move focus to its default action (TRY AGAIN on the error dialog, START or the first match option in the lobby, the first settings row) and give :focus-visible a clear 2 px signal-colour outline plus a fill change. Make the '01 You' identity marker look different from the focus style, for example a left bar only, no full outline.
- **After:** PASS: Error dialog focuses its way on (Try again) with the focus ring visible

### F05 · 'No game server answers this page' reads like a developer message, and Play Online still looks pressable

- **Where:** main-menu @ desktop, box [56, 524, 720, 212] in [main-menu@desktop.png](2026-11-05/captures/main-menu%40desktop.png)
- **Rule:** NEW
- **Severity:** medium · **Lens:** first-time
- **What:** A newcomer doesn't know what a 'game server' for 'this page' is or whether it's their fault. PLAY ONLINE stays at full size and full colour and is still enabled, so the first thing they're likely to press leads straight to the 'Server unreachable' dialog. It's also unclear whether Battle Royale (60 seats) needs that same server, because it's the only mode with no Online/Offline tag.
- **Evidence:** main-menu@desktop.dom.json: control 'PLAY ONLINE No game server answers this page' disabled:false; the Battle Royale meta is '60 seats · Zero Island · Solos, Duos or Trios' with no Offline tag, while Bot Match and Practice Range both say 'Offline'.
- **Fix:** Say it in player terms: 'Online play is down right now. Try Bot Match.' Dim PLAY ONLINE (and Battle Royale too if it needs the server), and tag every entry 'Online' or 'Offline' the same way.

### F08 · The lit destination differs from the others only by a thin coloured bar

- **Where:** main-menu @ desktop, box [56, 736, 720, 106] in [main-menu@desktop.png](2026-11-05/captures/main-menu%40desktop.png)
- **Rule:** COL-02, Never colour alone
- **Severity:** medium · **Lens:** accessibility
- **What:** Every destination row has a 4 px bar on its left edge. The lit row (Bot Match) differs only in that its bar is teal instead of grey. Its title colour, size, weight and background are the same as the other rows'. Across a 720 px wide row, a 4 px change in hue is easy to miss from couch distance and for players with colour-vision deficiency. DESIGN.md says hover and focus grow the bar and slide the title 10 px, but in the resting lit state that cue isn't visible.
- **Evidence:** main-menu@desktop.png: Bot Match row [56,736,720,106] has a teal bar at x=56; Play Online, Battle Royale and Practice Range have grey bars of the same width. Title colour is (243,241,234) on all four rows in main-menu@desktop.dom.json. The same holds on phone: the Bot Match row [16,561,358,93] differs only by bar colour.
- **Fix:** Give the lit state at least one cue that isn't hue: keep the 10 px title offset applied, widen the bar to 6–8 px, and/or add a faint row fill (about 8% white). Dim the unlit titles slightly so the lit one stands out by luminance as well as hue.
