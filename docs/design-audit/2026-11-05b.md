Ticket 24 (HUD, scoreboard, pause; all modes). 26 findings: 6 high, 16 medium, 4 low. After the fixes, 17 PASS and 9 FAIL. Measured contrast fails in the eight shots went from 20 to 0, and size fails from 67 to 0.

1. The phone scoreboard is now one full-width column. Before, Orange's columns spilled off the panel (F04).

## Details

### F01 · A phone player has no way to move, aim or fire

- **Where:** range-hud @ phone, box [0, 422, 390, 422] in [range-hud@phone.png](2026-11-05b/captures/range-hud%40phone.png)
- **Rule:** MENU-03, Controls are big enough to hit
- **Severity:** high · **Lens:** accessibility
- **What:** At 390x844 the game drops the player into the range and the bot match with only the desktop HUD. The DOM sample lists zero interactive controls on range-hud@phone and match-hud@phone, so there is no move stick, look area or fire button. The pause menu then lists keyboard keys (WASD, Left mouse, Shift Left, Control Left, Esc) as 'Controls' on a touch screen. A touch-only player can reach a match and do nothing there.
- **Evidence:** range-hud@phone.dom.json and match-hud@phone.dom.json: controls = [], focused = null. pause@phone: Controls panel rows 'WASD' (45,469), 'Left mouse' (45,501), 'Right mouse' (45,533), 'Shift Left' (45,565), 13 px bold.
- **Fix:** On coarse-pointer devices, either add a touch layer (left-half move stick, right-half look, fire/ADS/reload/jump buttons at least 44x44 with 8 px gaps, inside the safe area) or block entry with a clear 'Needs keyboard and mouse, or a controller' notice before a match starts. In the pause menu, show touch bindings instead of the keyboard table.
- **After:** FAIL: No touch controls: out of this ticket's scope (the phone HUD is laid out, not playable by touch)

### F02 · Score strip team labels fail contrast and size

- **Where:** match-hud @ desktop, box [804, 24, 312, 58] in [match-hud@desktop.png](2026-11-05b/captures/match-hud%40desktop.png)
- **Rule:** COL-01, Text contrast over the real background
- **Severity:** high · **Lens:** accessibility, hud, rules
- **What:** The team names and mode line under the score are 11 px and are drawn in a dim grey on a translucent plate, so they fail 4.5:1 even at the median background. The blue score digit (30 px bold) is dark blue on that plate and falls below 3:1 in the worst tenth. This strip is how the player knows which team is theirs.
- **Evidence:** match-hud@desktop '◆ Blue · you' 11 px, declared 6.19, p50 3.04, p10 1.97 (fail/fail); '▲ Orange' 11 px p10 2.67; 'TDM · 75' 11 px p10 2.91; blue '0' 30 px bold, declared 5.57, p10 2.79 (needs 3). Same failures on match-hud@phone ('◆ Blue · you' p10 2.05), scoreboard@desktop and scoreboard@phone. · contrast.md match-hud@desktop: '◆ Blue · you' [819,64,88,11] p10 1.97 fail; '▲ Orange' [1031,64,70,11] p10 2.67 fail; 'TDM · 75' [941,62,55,11] p10 2.91 fail; all 11 px (size fail). · contrast.md match-hud@desktop: '◆ Blue · you' p10 1.97 fail, '▲ Orange' 2.67 fail, 'TDM · 75' 2.91 fail, 'Health' p10 1.08; the screenshot shows 'Health' as a barely visible smudge at 51,975.
- **Fix:** Make the plate opaque (alpha 0.9 or more), set the labels to 12-13 px uppercase in the light text colour (180,186,197 gives 9.77 declared elsewhere), and lighten the blue score digit until it reaches 4.5:1 on the plate. Keep the ◆/▲ glyphs: they carry team identity without relying on colour.
- **After:** PASS: Team labels at 13 px on the flat strip
