<!-- summary:start -->
The audit that closed tickets 23 (touch controls) and 26 (onboarding): the first one with the touch layer up, on a phone held sideways (844x390, as the game is played) and upright (390x844, menus only), beside desktop. The phone shots were taken with real touches, so the thumb controls are in them. 40 findings; 26 pass in the After column, 14 are left with the reason beside each. Measured over the 50 shots, text failing contrast went from 135 to 13, and none of the 13 is text a player reads (kill-feed lines already faded out, two lines under a sticky footer, and the ping label on its old translucent plate).

The three fixes that matter most:

1. **A phone on its side showed half of each screen (F03, F04, F26, F27, F02).** The main menu opened on the two destinations that need a server, the pause menu hid the legend that names the touch buttons, and the end panel and the scoreboard lay on each other. Under 520 px of height the main menu now puts the name and the Rank right of the list, Pause puts its legend beside its actions, and the match's end is the panel alone, with the scoreboard a press away in its place.
2. **One line speaks at a time above the readouts on touch (F22, F23, F30, F41, F25).** The first-match hint was printed over the announcer's caption, both at 13 px. They share one slot now, the caption first, at 16 px, and the scoreboard is opaque and alone on the screen.
3. **Nothing on the sky, nothing on each other (F15, F02, F16, F20, F24).** The countdown and the result stand on plates, ally tags never overlap (the nearer ally keeps their name, edge tags step along the edge), and a ping's label has a solid plate.

Also fixed: every Settings tab shows on an upright phone (F01), Play Online with no server leads with Check again and explains the passphrase (F08, F09), a field never raises the keyboard by itself (F10), touch has a Fire hint (F21, in part), the Use prompt is a real button on touch (F18), HUD figures are tabular (F19), and the scoreboard's header says the goal (F36).

Left as they are, with their owners: the Profile screen's Reset placement and challenge wording (F07, F33) and the Battle Royale screen (F11, F12) are the owner's; the loadout's empty Weapon card (F14, F34) was the weapons session's; desktop slider height and 13 px sentences in Settings (F31, F32) the settings session's; desktop readouts over bright ground (F17) the owner's HUD work. Kept on purpose: the HUD size stays locked on a phone (F05). Low and open: F35 (eased), F37, F38. One finding of the merged 41 was dropped as not a fault: a button focused by script after a mouse click shows no ring in any browser, and it does for a keyboard or a pad.

Not covered by the captures: Battle Royale in play (Preparation, the drop, the map), Charge and Outposts, the spectator panel, a pad driving the menus, and any real phone. Every phone shot is Chrome's device emulation with touch events. Three fixes came after the After shots and are not in them: the 8 px between a picker's segments on touch (F13), the ping label's solid plate (F24) and the label on the range's recoil trace (F28).

### F01 · Upright phone settings hide the Audio and Accessibility tabs off-screen with no sign that more tabs exist

- **Where:** settings @ phone-upright, box [16, 87, 358, 44] in [settings@phone-upright.png](2026-11-10/captures/settings%40phone-upright.png)
- **Rule:** MENU-02, Always know where you are and how to go back
- **Severity:** high · **Lens:** accessibility
- **What:** A player holding the phone upright sees four tabs that end cleanly at the right margin and nothing that says the strip scrolls. Anyone looking for Audio or Accessibility, the players who most need to find it, has no cue that those pages exist. Landscape (settings@phone) at least shows a clipped 'ACCESSIB' as a hint; upright shows none.
- **Evidence:** settings@phone-upright controls: TOUCH [16,87,65,44], CONTROLS [83,87,92,44], VIDEO [312,87,61,44] ends at x=373; AUDIO starts at [375,87,62,44], past the 374 px content edge of a 390 px viewport. settings-hud@phone-upright has ACCESSIBILITY at [376,87,121,44], also fully outside. settings@phone shows the clipped tab at [751,10,125,44].
- **Fix:** On upright phones wrap the tabs onto two rows, or keep the sideways scroll but always leave half a tab cut at the right edge plus a fade or chevron so the strip reads as scrollable.
- **After:** PASS: All seven tabs show on three rows; nothing is off the screen.
