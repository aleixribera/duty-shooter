Contact sheet: [2026-11-13/sheet.html](2026-11-13/sheet.html) · before/after: [2026-11-13/compare.html](2026-11-13/compare.html) · contrast table: [2026-11-13/captures/contrast.md](2026-11-13/captures/contrast.md).

<!-- summary:start -->
The first audit of the Backlit restyle (ticket 114). No text fails contrast in play, and the low-light HUD reads. The three fixes that matter most, all made in the same release (see the After column):

1. **Focus you can find (F12, F18).** The half-lit edge was 2.9:1 against an unlit plate, and a focused lit button changed only from warm white to white. A pad player lost track of where they were. `--ds-light-half` is now `#b39d76` (4.6:1), and focus on a lit control adds a ring 4 px out.
2. **The way to play when the server is down (F01, F07, F08).** Squads that can't be picked looked live, with Solos fully lit. On a sideways phone, Local bots sat under the fold beneath a lit Check again. A shut picker is now unlit and dimmed, and on a phone with the server down, Play on comes first.
3. **The opening seconds on a short screen (F02, F24, F25, F27, F28).** The opening card covered the match bar, and the lit clock outshone the countdown. The clock's changing width also slid the whole bar. Now the card stands under the bar, the countdown number is lit while the clock rests, the clock holds the width of its longest face, and health and ammo shrink on short screens (the ammo plate only a little: F27 stays open). Note that the phone shots ran with a mouse: a real touch makes the page switch to its touch layout, which already placed these.

Not changed, for the director (`BACKLOG.md`): F16, where the loadout lights the chosen tile of every slot (DESIGN.md's "one lit plate per group"); the reviewer would light only choices the player made. Also F14/F15/F21, the sideways-phone Bot Match and settings that need a scroll. The 37 low findings stay open in this table.

Not covered by the captures: the kill moment's beats (frames in `.scratch/next-level/ui/backlit-screens/after/kill-*`), the gunsmith (the same screen as the loadout now), and touch mode on phones.

### F01 · With the server down, the way to play a Battle Royale (Local bots) is nowhere on the phone screen

- **Where:** royale @ phone, box [16, 74, 340, 28] in [royale@phone.png](2026-11-13/captures/royale%40phone.png)

- **Severity:** high · **Lens:** first-time
