## Duels

Two moderate bots, one primary each plus the pistol and a frag, start in sight of each other in a seeded yard with crates and walls, weapons drawn, and fight until one dies. Each cell is 240 duels: 120 seeded spots and bot pairs, each fought twice with the spots and personalities swapped. A draw counts half.
